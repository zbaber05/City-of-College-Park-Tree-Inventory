Every tree, counted.

A joint project of the City of College Park and the University of Maryland’s Department of Environmental Science and Technology. This is a tree-by-tree inventory: where each tree stands, how big it is, how much shade it casts, and how it’s doing. So far we’ve only measured trees with a trunk diameter (DBH) of 20 cm or more, so smaller trees aren’t counted yet.
